Keyboard shortcuts

EnterEdit the selected cell; press again to save & move right
Tab / Shift+TabSave and move to the next / previous cell
Arrow keysMove between cells (or move the text cursor while editing)
Shift+ArrowExtend the selected range
Click + dragSelect a range of cells
Ctrl+CCopy the selected cell(s)
Ctrl+VPaste into the selected cell(s)
Ctrl+DFill down from the row above (or the top row of a range)
Delete / BackspaceClear the selected cell(s)
EscCancel editing, or collapse a range to one cell
Right-click a rowInsert a row above/below, or delete the row
Shift+?Show this list
